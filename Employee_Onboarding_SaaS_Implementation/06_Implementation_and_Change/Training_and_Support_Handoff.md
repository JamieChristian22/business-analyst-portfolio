# Training and support handoff
## HR quick guide
Create request; check validation errors; submit; monitor approval status; correct rejected items; escalate aged requests.
## Manager quick guide
Review identity, start date, role, access rationale; approve/reject with reason; respond within agreed SLA.
## IT runbook handoff
Monitor exception queue; use correlation ID; classify failure; verify idempotency; never bypass approvals; record resolution and notify HR.
## Training validation
Each role completes a sandbox walkthrough and signs a checklist; measure unresolved support questions before go-live.
## Support matrix
Tier 1: intake/help desk; Tier 2: integration/IAM; Tier 3: SaaS vendor; Security: privileged-access incidents. Contact names and escalation times pending.
