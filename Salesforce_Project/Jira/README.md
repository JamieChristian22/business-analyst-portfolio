# ☁️ Salesforce CRM Analytics Project – Jira (Agile Execution)

## 📌 Overview

This section showcases how Agile (Scrum) methodologies were applied to manage and deliver a Salesforce CRM analytics solution. Jira was used to simulate a real-world environment where business requirements were translated into actionable features supporting sales operations and customer relationship management.

The focus is on backlog management, user story development, sprint execution, and CRM analytics delivery.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To design and deliver a Salesforce-based analytics solution that provides insights into:

* Sales pipeline performance
* Opportunity tracking and forecasting
* Customer engagement metrics
* Lead conversion rates
* KPI dashboards for sales operations

---

## ⚙️ Agile Methodology (Scrum)

* Framework: Scrum
* Sprint Duration: 2 Weeks
* Tool: Jira
* Supporting Tools: Salesforce, Excel, Power BI

---

## 📝 Sample User Stories

### 📌 User Story 1: Sales Pipeline Dashboard

**As a** sales manager
**I want** to view the sales pipeline in Salesforce
**So that** I can monitor deal progress and forecast revenue

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* Pipeline stages displayed clearly
* Opportunities categorized by stage
* Forecast revenue calculated dynamically
* Filters available by region and sales rep

---

### 📌 User Story 2: Lead Conversion Tracking

**As a** business analyst
**I want** to track lead-to-opportunity conversion rates
**So that** I can evaluate sales effectiveness

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* Conversion rate calculated automatically
* Visualization shows trends over time
* Breakdown by campaign source

---

### 📌 User Story 3: Opportunity Performance Insights

**As a** sales executive
**I want** to analyze opportunity win/loss rates
**So that** I can improve sales strategy

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* Win/loss ratio displayed
* Historical performance trends included
* Drill-down capability for detailed insights
