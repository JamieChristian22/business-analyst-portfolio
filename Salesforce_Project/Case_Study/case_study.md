# Sales Pipeline Analytics Case Study

## Problem
Sales team lacked visibility into pipeline performance and revenue forecasting.

## Approach
Analyzed CRM dataset, calculated KPIs, built weighted revenue model, and performed scenario analysis.

## Key Metrics
- Total Pipeline: $164000
- Weighted Pipeline: $83550.0
- Avg Deal Size: $16400.0
- Closed Lost: $15000

## Findings
- Proposal stage drives $67000 in revenue
- $15000 revenue lost indicates inefficiencies
- Early funnel has low conversion potential

## Business Impact
- Improving win rate from 40% to 55% generates +$10050.0
- Increasing deal size by 10% raises pipeline to $180400.0

## Recommendations
- Focus on Proposal and Negotiate stages
- Improve qualification process
- Use weighted forecasting
