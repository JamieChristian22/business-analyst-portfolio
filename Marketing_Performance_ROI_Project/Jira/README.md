# 📈 Marketing Performance ROI Project – Jira (Agile Execution)

## 📌 Overview

This section demonstrates how Agile (Scrum) methodologies were used to manage and deliver a Marketing Performance ROI analytics solution. Jira was used to simulate a real-world product development environment focused on tracking campaign effectiveness and return on investment.

The project highlights backlog management, sprint execution, and delivery of data-driven marketing insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To design and deliver an analytics solution that measures and optimizes marketing ROI across multiple campaigns by analyzing:

* Campaign performance metrics
* Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
* Conversion rates
* Revenue attribution
* ROI by channel

---

## ⚙️ Agile Methodology (Scrum)

* Framework: Scrum
* Sprint Duration: 2 Weeks
* Tool: Jira
* Supporting Tools: Excel, SQL, Power BI

---

## 🧩 Role & Responsibilities (Business Analyst / Product Owner)

* Defined marketing analytics product vision and ROI goals
* Gathered and documented business requirements from stakeholders
* Created and prioritized product backlog
* Translated requirements into epics and user stories
* Wrote detailed acceptance criteria
* Collaborated with stakeholders (marketing, analytics, leadership)
* Ensured backlog readiness for sprint planning

---

## 📝 Sample User Stories

### 📌 User Story 1: Campaign ROI Dashboard

**As a** marketing manager
**I want** to view ROI for each campaign
**So that** I can determine which campaigns are most profitable

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* ROI calculated as (Revenue - Cost) / Cost
* Dashboard displays ROI by campaign
* Filters available by channel and timeframe
* Data refreshes automatically

---

### 📌 User Story 2: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Analysis

**As a** business analyst
**I want** to calculate CAC by channel
**So that** I can identify cost-efficient acquisition strategies

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* CAC calculated per campaign/channel
* Visualization compares CAC across channels
* Historical trends available

---

### 📌 User Story 3: Conversion Rate Tracking

**As a** marketing stakeholder
**I want** to track conversion rates
**So that** I can optimize campaign performance

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* Conversion rate displayed by campaign
* Funnel stages visualized
* Drill-down capability enabled
