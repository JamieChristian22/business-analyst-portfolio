# 🛒 Amazon Marketplace Analytics Project

## 📊 Executive Summary
This project delivers a **data-driven analysis of Amazon marketplace performance**, enabling stakeholders to identify revenue drivers, optimize pricing strategy, and improve seller performance.

The solution integrates **Excel modeling, SQL analysis, and dashboard visualization** to transform raw marketplace data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🚀 Business Problem
Amazon marketplace performance data is often:
- Fragmented across multiple sources
- Difficult to analyze at scale
- Lacking clear KPI alignment

This results in:
- Poor pricing decisions
- Low visibility into seller performance
- Missed revenue opportunities

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze revenue, profit, and product performance
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products
- Evaluate pricing and margin trends
- Build an executive-ready dashboard for decision-making

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- Top 20% of products drive majority of revenue (Pareto effect)
- Certain categories have high revenue but low profit margins
- Pricing inconsistencies impact conversion rates
- Seasonal trends influence marketplace performance

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Excel** – Financial modeling & scenario analysis
